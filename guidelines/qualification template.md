# Qualification page template

## Hero statement

Create five hero statement options. Each option must:

- be one sentence of no more than 25 words
- introduce the qualification in an inviting, summary tone
- not mention the qualification by name
- focus on what the qualification helps students learn, achieve, or experience while studying at Otago

## Overview

Up to 80 words.

Provide a succinct summary of:

- what the qualification involves
- what students study
- the opportunities or pathways it can lead to

## Marketing block 1 — how students learn

**Heading:** Create five heading options of 6 to 10 words. These should summarise how students learn at Otago and what makes studying this qualification distinctive.

**Content:** Up to 75 words. Highlight Otago's distinctive strengths such as teaching expertise, research environment, facilities, learning community, or reputation and graduate outcomes. Focus on what makes studying this qualification at Otago unique.

## Programme details introduction

Up to 100 words.

Write a short introductory paragraph explaining how the qualification programme is structured. Briefly describe:

- how students progress through the qualification
- any flexibility in subjects or majors
- how the programme builds knowledge and skills over time

## Marketing block 2 — what makes Otago distinctive

**Heading:** Create five heading options of 6 to 10 words. These should summarise what makes studying this qualification at Otago distinctive.

**Content:** Up to 75 words. Highlight Otago's distinctive strengths such as teaching expertise, research environment, facilities, learning community, or reputation and graduate outcomes. Do not duplicate content from marketing block 1.

---

## Final checks

Before submitting your draft:

- Confirm tone matches the audience level (undergraduate or postgraduate).
- Verify content accuracy and avoid repetition across sections.
- Replace any missing details with [Generated Content] and flag uncertain details with [Check].
- Ensure language flows naturally, reads easily, and feels human.
